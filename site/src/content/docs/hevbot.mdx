Everything the factory does on the always-on host, it does as hevbot. That's
the feature. Plenty of tools give you background agents. This one gives you a
teammate with its own account.

## One rule

**A session acts as whoever its host is logged in as, never as who asked for
it.**

| Host | Acts as | Good for |
|---|---|---|
| The always-on Mac | hevbot: its own `gh`, git author, subscriptions and vault | Anything long, heavy, overnight, or run while you're away |
| Your laptop | You | Short parallel work you want under your own name, and overflow |

To get a commit under your own name, run the work on your laptop. To get it
under hevbot's, run it on the always-on host. A session can never borrow the
other identity, which is why the always-on host holds none of your logins.

## Why its own account

- **Review.** hevbot's pull requests show up as a colleague's, so you review them
  like a colleague's. You never have to wonder which of your own commits you
  didn't write.
- **Reach.** hevbot can reach exactly what its vault grants: the repos it has been
  invited to, the Linear workspace it has joined, the subscriptions it
  holds. Access is scoped per account, not per prompt.
- **Revocation.** To take something away, revoke it from hevbot. To stop
  everything, revoke hevbot. Nothing it did was done as you, so nothing of
  yours needs rotating.

## Running with permissions off

Sessions run `claude --permission-mode bypassPermissions`, or codex with
approvals off. They run shell commands, commit, push and open pull requests
without asking. The account boundary above is one half of what makes that
safe. The other half is the [line](/docs/lines) container each session
runs in.

## Where hevbot shows up

- **GitHub:** as the author of every commit and pull request from the always-on
  host.
- **The board:** as the author of what sessions post. You post as yourself,
  from the laptop.
- **The dashboard:** as the "acts as" column on [Hosts](/docs/dashboard).

Your own copy is called whatever you name the account. hevbot is ours.
